# Silent Action (Silent Auction)

**Internal auction**: Goods and services offered only by/for members.  
Members can be generous with their offerings and wise with their bidding. 

**External auction**: Goods and services offered to surrounding community.  
Engagement with their silent auctions as well, for example through their fundraising events. 

All auctions are silent.

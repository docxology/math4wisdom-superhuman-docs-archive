# Knowledge Tools

For knowledge engineering, I think it is meaningful to have:

- a database of ways of figuring things out (can be [https://coda.io/d/_d0SvdI3KSto/_suTEdstt](https://coda.io/d/_d0SvdI3KSto/_suTEdstt), cognitive models, epistemic portraits, etc). 

- a collection of systematizations for all manner of disciplines and personalities (*can be* *[https://coda.io/d/_d0SvdI3KSto/_sujqBh-1](https://coda.io/d/_d0SvdI3KSto/_sujqBh-1)* *and beyond*). 

I am curious to know how useful or meaningful or helpful this is.  

If it is fruitful, then we can work together to improve these sketches so that we are happy with them.  In doing so, we have a basis for appreciating the reality of basic structures - the twosome (relating the conscious and the unconscious), the threesome (the learning cycle), the foursome (levels of knowledge: whether, what, how, why) as well as more sophisticated structures of the form 4+4 and 4+6.  

This makes Wondrous Wisdom socially real.

# Situationism

<columns>
[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situationist_International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situationist_International)

The **Situationist International** (**SI**) was an [international](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proletarian_internationalism) organization of social revolutionaries made up of [avant-garde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avant-garde) artists, intellectuals, and [political theorists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_philosophy). It was prominent in Europe from its formation in 1957 to its dissolution in 1972.

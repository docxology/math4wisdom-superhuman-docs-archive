I noted that we could look at the assumption built into the question.  The notion of conditions itself suggest four zones: 

- a space within conditions, 

- the conditions beyond that space, 

- coming from the conditions into the space, 

- and going out of the space into the conditions. 

These zones are familiar to the perception-action loop of Active Inference (organism, environment, perception, action) and they are also Spiritism, Materialism, Realism, Idealism, accordingly, as 4 worldviews in Steiner's system of 12 worldviews.  Furthermore, the idea of symbols suggest pairs of zones, for the signifier and the signified, and there are six such pairs, assuming the are ordered in a particular direction. (Otherwise, there are 12 if you can go in either direction.)  This is a familiar 4+6 structure of Wondrous Wisdom which also matches up with the 4 vertices and 6 edges of a tetrahedron.

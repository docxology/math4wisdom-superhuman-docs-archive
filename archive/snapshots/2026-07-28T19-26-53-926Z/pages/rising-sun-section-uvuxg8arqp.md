# Rising Sun

<columns>
Franklin said that “in the course of the session” he had often looked at the sun behind the President (Washington) without being able to tell whether it was rising or setting. With the Constitution finally agreed and being signed, he concluded “but now at length I have the happiness to know, that it is a rising and not a setting sun.”

*The* ***American Party****, known as the* ***Native American Party*** *before 1855 and colloquially referred to as the* ***Know Nothings****, or the* ***Know Nothing Party****, was an* *[Old Stock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Stock_Americans)* *[nativist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nativism_in_United_States_politics)* *political movement in the United States in the 1850s. Members of the movement were required to say "I know nothing" whenever they were asked about its specifics by outsiders, providing the group with its colloquial name.*

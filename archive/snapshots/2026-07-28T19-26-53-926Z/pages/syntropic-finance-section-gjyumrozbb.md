# Syntropic Finance

Markus Patz

[https://www.e-c-o.net/wiki/Econet/SyntropicFinance](https://www.e-c-o.net/wiki/Econet/SyntropicFinance)

SyntroFi is a term that was coined by [Marcus Petz](https://www.e-c-o.net/wiki/Econet/MarcusPetz) based on his observations of the work of [Diogo Jorge](https://www.e-c-o.net/wiki/Econet/DiogoJorge) and experiences at the CoFi Week in Belgium. He presented the idea in a paper given at the RAMICS Rome conference in November 2024. [Stephanie Rearick](https://www.e-c-o.net/wiki/Econet/StephanieRearick) of HUMANS has expressed an interest in developing the concept.

# First Mind Bike Trip

Andrius Kulikauskas: I share photos from my bicycle trip from my village home in Eičiūnai, Lithuania to the tip of Latvia and back.  I made this trip to be physically fit, to make the most of summer and be emotionally ready for another winter, to feed my unconscious “first mind” with opportunities and experiences that it can play with, to feel that I can live on the road indefinitely with my bike, tent, laptop, and modest expense.  I feel liberated!
